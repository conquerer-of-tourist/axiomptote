# Axiomptote

## About

This is a research project I've been thinking about for over a year now (as of April 2026), and I finally got the courage to make a repository for it. The name had to sound cool, and it needed to be decently relevant to the research topic, Asymptote diagrams. So, I chose a blend of Axiom and Asymptote to make Axiomptote. My first goal is to gather a large and sufficiently diverse database of Asymptote diagrams and their corresponding programs. Then, I need to figure out training logistics -- I obviously don't have much money, so I'll deal with all that later on.
